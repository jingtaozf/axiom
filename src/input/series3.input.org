Question by Nicolas Francois =<nicolas.francois@free.fr>=: 
Is there any way to obtain the decomposition
in simple elements of a fraction of the form:
\[\frac{1}{(1-x)(1-x^2)(1-x^5)}\]

Martin Rubey =<martin.rubey@math.uni-hannover.de>= replied:
#+NAME: *
#+begin_src common-lisp :noweb yes

--S 1 of 4
SAEs5 := SAE(FRAC INT, UP(s5,FRAC INT),s5^2-5)
--E 1

--S 2 of 4
p:UP(x,SAEs5) := (x^5-1)*(x^2-1)*(x-1)
--E 2

--S 3 of 4
factor p
--E 3

--S 4 of 4
partialFraction(1/p,x)
--E 4

)spool 
)lisp (bye)
